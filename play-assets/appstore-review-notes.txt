provisioned on our servers, so no setup is required on your side.

- In the field labelled "Contrasena", type: Review2025!
- Tap the login button.

This account has the administrator role and full access to every module:

Nutrition.

Two more accounts are available if you want to see the app from a

- 900000002 (coordinator)

There is no 2-step verification, OTP, QR code or location restriction.
Fingerprint/Face ID login is optional: it only appears after a user turns it on
inside the app, so a fresh install always shows the user/password form.
You will not be asked security questions with these accounts.

2) GUIDELINE 3.2 - BUSINESS (DISTRIBUTION METHOD)

This app is designed for internal company use and is not intended for the
general public. Following Guideline 3.2 - Business, the app is configured as
Unlisted in App Store Connect. Our Unlisted App request was approved under case
number 102749730900.

The unlisted URL will be distributed only to authorized users in our client
organizations. Access is further restricted by authenticated sign-in and
role-based module permissions: accounts cannot be self-created, they are
provisioned by each organization's administrator.

3) ABOUT THE NUTRITION MODULE

The Nutrition module is a record-keeping tool for nutrition professionals
employed by our client institutions. It stores patient records, ICD-11
diagnoses entered by the professional, body mass index calculated from weight
and height, and diet plans. It does not diagnose or treat on its own and it is
not a regulated medical device. Access is limited to authorized professional
staff within each organization.
